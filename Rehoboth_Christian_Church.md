## Statement of Faith

### God
There is one God, who is infinitely perfect, existing eternally in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.  
(*Isaiah 44:6; Matthew 5:48; Matthew 3:16-17; Matthew 28:19*)

### Jesus
Jesus Christ is true God and true man. He was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary.  
He died on the cross as a substitutionary sacrifice. He rose from the dead, is at the right hand of God, and will come again.  
(*Philippians 2:6-11; Matthew 1:18; 1 Corinthians 15:3-5*)

### Holy Spirit
The Holy Spirit is a divine Person who indwells, guides, teaches, and empowers the believer.  
(*John 14:16-17; John 16:7-11*)

### Bible
The Old and New Testaments are the inerrant, inspired Word of God and are the only rule of Christian faith and practice.  
(*2 Timothy 3:16; 2 Peter 1:20-21*)

### Sin
Humankind was created in God’s image but fell through disobedience. All are born with a sinful nature and can only be saved through Jesus Christ.  
(*Genesis 1:27; Romans 8:8*)

### Freedom from Sin
Salvation is only through Jesus Christ by repentance and faith, resulting in regeneration, justification, sanctification, and eternal life.  
(*Titus 3:5-7; John 1:12*)

### Christian Living
It is God’s will that each believer be sanctified, empowered for holy living, and effective service through being filled with the Holy Spirit.  
(*Romans 12:1-2; Galatians 5:16-25*)

### Healing
Provision for physical healing is made in the redemptive work of Jesus.  
(*Matthew 8:16-17; James 5:13-16*)

### Church
The universal Church is made of all believers in Christ. The local church is a visible expression, joined together for worship, ordinances, prayer, fellowship, and mission.  
(*Ephesians 1:22-23; Acts 2:41-47*)

### Life After Death
There will be a bodily resurrection of the just to life and the unjust to judgment.  
(*1 Corinthians 15:20-23; 2 Thessalonians 1:7-10*)

### Second Coming of Christ
The personal and visible return of Jesus Christ is imminent and is the blessed hope of believers, inspiring holy living and sacrificial service.  
(*1 Thessalonians 4:13-17; Titus 2:11-14*)
